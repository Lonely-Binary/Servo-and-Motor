PCA9685 16-Channel 12-Bit PWM Servo Driver -- net list

Extracted from the fabrication data (FlyingProbeTesting.json inside the Gerber
archive). Vias and bare test pads are omitted; only component pins are listed.
Net names beginning with a designator suffix (A0_SEL, VIN_RAW, ...) were
unnamed in the source and have been given readable names here.

  U1   PCA9685PW, TSSOP-28          U2   KF301-5.0-2P screw terminal
  U3   ME6212C50, 5 V LDO           U4   ME6217C33, 3.3 V LDO
  U12  6-pin female socket          H3   6-pin right-angle male header
  Q1   AO3401A P-channel MOSFET     USB1 USB Type-C, 6-pin, power only
  H1/H2/H4/H5   PWM pin row         H6-H9   V+ pin row
  H10-H13       GND pin row         RN1-RN4 220 ohm arrays
  RN5/RN6       10 kilohm arrays    A0-A5/S1/S2 solder jumpers

Resistor arrays are 4-element 0603x8: R1 = pins 1 and 8, R2 = 2 and 7,
R3 = 3 and 6, R4 = 4 and 5.
